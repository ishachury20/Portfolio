Overview

Genesis is a community-based freight and moving platform designed to connect customers with independent transporters across Latin America, acting as a sustainable alternative to historically fragmented networks.

I owned and led the interface design for Genesis while the product was still undefined, translating an ambitious, largely unscoped vision into a usable, trustworthy platform — shaping the core user flows, the visual system, and the interactions that would hold the experience together as the product itself was still being figured out.

The final product vision evolved from an early concept into a complete, interactive booking experience supported by a robust component system, successfully establishing the foundation as the team prepared for development.
